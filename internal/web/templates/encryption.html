Data at rest

{{if .On}}

● Encrypted. Your database and backups are unreadable without your key file or recovery key.

Uploads wait briefly in the data folder's temp space while they import, then are deleted. Passwords and keys in your Portainer stack are stored by Portainer, so move them into Settings and remove them from the stack.

{{else}}

● Not encrypted. Anyone who gets your data volume can read it.

  1. On the server, make a key outside the data volume: 
    sudo mkdir -p /opt/backtrackarr-secrets
openssl rand -hex 32 | sudo tee /opt/backtrackarr-secrets/backtrackarr.key > /dev/null
sudo chown 65532:65532 /opt/backtrackarr-secrets/backtrackarr.key
sudo chmod 400 /opt/backtrackarr-secrets/backtrackarr.key
  2. In your stack, add this to the app's volumes: 
    - /opt/backtrackarr-secrets/backtrackarr.key:/run/secrets/backtrackarr.key:ro
  3. Add this to its environment, then update the stack: 
    ENCRYPTION_KEY_FILE: "/run/secrets/backtrackarr.key"
  4. Come back here and save the recovery key.
{{end}}
