Netflix from your signed-in browser

  1. Install the BackTrackarr scrobbler (needs the Tampermonkey extension).
  2. Open Viewing activity and pick your profile.
  3. Click Send all to BackTrackarr at the bottom right.

Your password stays with Netflix. Live plays are recorded automatically once the scrobbler is installed.
